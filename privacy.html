Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What we collect

When you use Tailor, you voluntarily provide the following information to generate your resume:

  • Full name
  • Email address — used to deliver your PDF resume
  • Phone number (optional)
  • LinkedIn / portfolio URL (optional)
  • Work experience, education, skills, projects, awards, and references — the content of your resume
  • Job descriptions you paste to tailor your resume

We do not collect payment information, location data, contacts, browsing history, or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Generate a personalised, ATS-optimised resume PDF
  • Deliver your PDF to the email address you provide
  • Store your profile locally on your device so you don't have to re-enter it

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond resume generation.

3. Where your data goes

When you tap "Tailor My Resume," your information is sent to our secure automation pipeline:

  • n8n (our automation server) — orchestrates the resume generation process. Your data passes through this server but is not permanently stored there.
  • Google Gemini AI — rewrites and optimises your resume content. Google's data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • LaTeX compiler (ytotech.com) — converts your resume to a PDF. Your data is processed momentarily and not retained.
  • Gmail (via Google) — delivers your PDF to the email address you provided.

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

4. Local storage

Your profile (name, email, phone, resume content) is stored locally on your device only using encrypted device storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore for sensitive fields, and app-sandboxed storage for resume content).

We do not maintain a server-side database of user profiles. If you uninstall the app or delete your account, your data is permanently removed from your device.

5. Data retention

Your data is stored locally on your device for as long as you use the app. It is deleted immediately when you:

  • Tap "Delete My Account" in Settings
  • Uninstall the app (device storage is cleared)

Data sent to our automation pipeline for resume generation is processed in real time and is not retained beyond the duration of a single generation request.

6. Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access — view the data stored in the app at any time (it's all in your profile)
  • Delete — remove all your data via Settings → Delete My Account, or by emailing us
  • Correct — edit any information directly in the app
  • Portability — your data is visible and editable within the app at all times
  • Opt out of sale — we do not sell your data, period

To request deletion of any data that may have passed through our pipeline, email us at the address below.

7. Children

Tailor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. AI-generated content

Tailor uses Google Gemini AI to rewrite and optimise your resume content. Your data is submitted to Gemini for each generation request. The output is generated content — please review it for accuracy before submitting job applications. We are not responsible for inaccuracies introduced by the AI.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact us
